Beyond the Institutional Framework

Welcome to the Virtual Dungeon, the personal website of Prof. Jaderick P. Pabico, a member of the Faculty of the Institute of Computer Science (ICS), University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB). This website was created to provide a venue for presenting Jaderick's work, ideas, and perspectives that extend beyond the limitations of his institutional webpage. While the latter is restricted to simple academic information, this space allows for the expression of his academic freedom—the inclusion of personal views, current reflections, and projects that may not always align with institutional positions. Content is organized into Teaching, Service, Research, Administration, Awards, Media, CV, and Personal.
